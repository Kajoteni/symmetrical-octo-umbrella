The Writer

A quester,
not a settler.

I write to think. These essays are the residue of questions I couldn't stop asking — about God, about the texture of ordinary life, systems of power, how and why.

Not a pundit. Not a theologian by degree. Just someone convinced that serious thought deserves serious prose — and that the examined life is worth writing down.
